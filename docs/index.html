Questions

Is TapeIt really free?

Yes. No watermark, no time limit and no account. Drive uploads use your own Google storage.

Will people on my Zoom call see the toolbar?

No. The toolbar is hidden from screen sharing and from your recordings by default. Your drawings and the webcam bubble stay visible. You can turn the toolbar back on in the menu.

What does TapeIt do with my recordings?

Nothing you don't ask for. Recordings stay on your Mac. TapeIt uploads only the files you choose, only to your own Drive. Read the privacy policy.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 15 Sequoia or later, on Apple silicon and Intel. TapeIt asks for Screen Recording permission the first time, and for camera and microphone only if you turn them on.

Can I export a GIF?

Yes. The editor trims the clip and exports MP4 or GIF, with optional zoom toward your clicks.
