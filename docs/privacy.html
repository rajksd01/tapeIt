TapeIt Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

What TapeIt collects

Nothing. TapeIt has no servers, no analytics and no accounts of its own.

Your recordings

Recordings stay on your Mac, in the folder you choose. TapeIt reads your screen, camera and microphone only while you record, and only with the macOS permissions you grant.

Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive, TapeIt uploads recordings you choose directly from your Mac to your own Drive. It requests only the drive.file scope, so it can see only files it created. It never reads other files in your Drive.

TapeIt makes uploaded files viewable by anyone with the link, so you can share them. You can change sharing or delete files in Google Drive at any time.

The sign-in token is stored in your Mac's Keychain. Choose "Disconnect" in TapeIt to remove it, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

TapeIt's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
